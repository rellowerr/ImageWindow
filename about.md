# ImageWindow

**ImageWindow** allows you to display any custom image, photo, reference, route cheat sheet, or meme directly on your screen while playing levels or creating art in the editor!

## ✨ Features

- **Built-in Image Library**: Add and import photos directly in-game or drop files into the mod's images directory (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.bmp`, `.webp`).
- **Interactive Drag Mode**: Drag and move the image anywhere on your screen with your mouse or touch in real-time!
- **3x3 Presets Grid**: Quick alignment buttons (Top-Left, Top-Right, Center, Bottom-Right, etc.).
- **Full Transform Controls**: 
  - Scale slider (0.1x to 3.0x)
  - Opacity slider (5% to 100%)
  - Rotation slider (0° to 360°)
  - Flip Horizontal & Flip Vertical toggles
- **Translucent HUD Window**: Optional dark sleek HUD frame with border.
- **Smart Hiding**:
  - Hide automatically on pause
  - Hide automatically on death
  - Show only in Practice Mode
- **Level Editor Support**: View reference images while creating level art.
- **Pause Menu Access**: Quick-access button conveniently located in the pause menu.

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Pause any level to open the **Pause Menu**.
2. Click the **ImageWindow** palette button.
3. In the **Library** tab, click **+ Add Image** or **Folder** to import images.
4. Select your active image, then switch to **HUD & Size** to customize size, position, and opacity.
